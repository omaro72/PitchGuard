# Scenario 7: Promotional and spam-like language

All names, organizations, publications, facts, and URLs in this scenario are fictional. The expected signals are test expectations, not recorded results.

## What this tests

A relevant product announcement surrounded by unsupported superlatives, guarantees, pressure, and a poor call to action. This should exercise non-critical PR risk rules.

## Campaign

- **Company name:** Solstice Pantry
- **Target audience:** Consumers interested in household meal planning
- **Announcement:** Solstice Pantry is releasing a fictional meal-planning application with shared lists and weekly calendar features.

## Supporting evidence

### Evidence E1

- **ID:** E1
- **Source:** Solstice Pantry fictional product brief
- **Content:** The application includes shared grocery lists, a weekly meal calendar, and recipe tagging. No market ranking, growth claim, or outcome guarantee has been verified.
- **Confidential:** No

## Journalist

- **Name:** Casey Monroe
- **Publication:** Everyday App Guide
- **Beat / topics covered:** Consumer applications, household organization tools, and practical product reviews

## Recent coverage

### Coverage C1

- **ID:** C1
- **Title:** Choosing a useful household planning app
- **Summary:** A fictional guide focused on clear features, realistic claims, and hands-on usefulness.
- **Publication date:** 2026-08-24
- **URL:** https://example.test/everyday-apps/planning-tools

## Pitch

```text
URGENT: You cannot miss the world's best and fastest-growing meal app. Solstice Pantry is a revolutionary platform guaranteed to eliminate meal-planning stress for every household. Your readers need this immediately, so confirm coverage today before this exclusive disappears.
```

## Expected review signals

- The leadership, growth, and guarantee claims should be `UNSUPPORTED`.
- The risk reviewer should identify unsupported superlatives, misleading certainty, excessive promotion, or spam-like pressure.
- The call to action is aggressive rather than reasonable.
- The likely decision is at least `REVISE`; exact categories and severity may vary.
